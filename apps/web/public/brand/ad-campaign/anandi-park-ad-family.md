# Anandi Park — Family/Couple Cinematic Ad ("Apna Kuch Toh Ho")
Variant built with the TOW SOP — **Concept 2**, the emotional route. A young couple
walk their own open plot and imagine the home they'll build. Cinematic VO on
**Veo 3.1**, images on **Nano Banana Pro**. VO recorded separately.

> Two characters = roughly twice the drift. The defenses below (separate identity
> blocks, a differentiation line, exact person counts) are mandatory here.

---

## SCRIPT (38s cinematic VO, Hinglish, ~100 words)
Hook: *(overheard)* "Kiraye ke ghar ki chaabi kabhi apni nahi lagti." — VISUAL: keys in a rented door.
```
(0-4s)   VO: "Kiraye ke ghar ki chaabi... kabhi apni nahi lagti."     VISUAL: keys turn in a rented flat door; muted interior. [thumbnail]

(17-24s) VO: (silence 2s) then "Yahaan hum ghar banayenge. Apna."     VISUAL: they stand on their open plot; he points, she smiles.
(24-31s) VO: "Gated, chaalees-feet roads, Kharadi pachchees minute.   VISUAL: pans — entry gate, central garden, a sample bungalow.
         Booking sirf das percent."
(31-38s) VO: "Anandi Park. Yahaan se ghar shuru hota hai."            VISUAL: couple on plot at sunset; end frame gate + logo + phone.
```
