## STAGE 1 — FOUNDATION

**FOUNDATION:** Anandi Park — 84 residential plots by Rich-Land Developers, Bakori/Wagholi, Pune East, from ₹18 Lakh.

**Assumptions [ASSUMED]:** target is Pune salaried/IT buyers & small investors, 28–45; objective is site-visit bookings; language Hinglish; platform Instagram Reels 9:16.

**WHO IT'S FOR:** *Aarav, 33, Kharadi IT professional, lives on rent in a 2BHK.* "Flat toh le lenge, par apna kuch nahi lagta." "Zameen hoti toh value badhti rehti." Weekend drives out east looking at what he can actually afford.

**THE PAIN:** Priced out of Pune flats that don't appreciate; rent feels like money burnt; wants something real and his own, without a 60-lakh loan.

**THE PROMISE:** Own a clear-title piece of land in a growing corridor for the price of a flat's down payment — build when ready, watch the value climb.

**THE ENEMY:** The belief that "a flat is the only safe first purchase" — and depreciating apartments in oversupplied belts.

**PROOF:** Clear, marketable titles; gated layout with 30 & 40 ft roads; 25 min to Kharadi; 10% booking + bank loans. (Appreciation figures are illustrative — mark [VERIFY] in any spoken claim.)

**ANGLE OPTIONS:**
- **A. "Land vs Flat":** the contrarian-truth angle — buildings age, land grows. Message: *own the ground, not the ceiling.*
- **B. "Apna Kuch":** the identity/emotion angle — the quiet pride of standing on your own soil. Message: *paisa flat mein nahi, apni zameen mein.*
- **C. "The Smart 10%":** the demonstration/offer angle — book at 10%, EMI the rest, value keeps rising. Message: *entry so easy it's foolish to keep waiting.*

**Recommended: A ("Land vs Flat"),** because it reframes the whole category in the first 3 seconds and gives the film a clear argument the visuals can prove — the strongest scroll-stopper for an investment-minded Pune audience.

## STAGE 2 — CONCEPT & FORMAT

**CONCEPT 1: "Land vs Flat"** (cinematic VO; runs on Veo 3.1; duration set at Stage 3)
- **Insight:** People chase flats because they feel safe, but a flat is the one asset that starts dying the day you buy it.
- **Logline:** A young professional stops circling flat brochures and walks onto his own open plot at golden hour, as a VO reframes what "owning" really means.
- **Beats:** rent/flat frustration → the drive out east → standing on his own land → the value truth → book a visit.
- **Scroll-stop (first 2s):** a hand closing a flat brochure, then an aerial reveal of open plotted land.
- **Product role:** the land appears by second 3 (aerial), hero end frame is the entry gate + logo card.
- **Ogilvy test:** remove Anandi Park and the film has nothing to stand on — the plot *is* the story.

**CONCEPT 2: "Apna Kuch Toh Ho"** (cinematic VO, emotional) — a father and son walk the plot imagining the home they'll build; softer, family-led.

**CONCEPT 3: "The 10% Move"** (UGC-style advisor to camera) — a friendly advisor explains how booking at 10% + EMI beats waiting; more direct-response, less premium.

**Recommended: Concept 1,** because it carries the recommended angle, is the most premium and most "Reel-stopping," and its argument is fully provable with your existing aerial + site footage.
