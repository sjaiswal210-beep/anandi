# Anandi Park — UGC Talking-Head Ad (Omni Flash)
Variant of the ad package, built with the TOW SOP. This is the **direct-response
UGC** version: one relatable advisor talks to camera, phone-video energy. Best for
**Click-to-WhatsApp ads** and Reels/feed, where trust and a fast pitch beat polish.

Runs on **Omni Flash** (Google Flow) — it speaks natively with your written voice
and accent, so the dialogue is baked into the clip (no separate VO). Images on
**Nano Banana Pro**.

---

## FOUNDATION / ANGLE
Same brand foundation as the hero package. Angle here = **C. "The Smart 10%"** —
demonstration + offer: booking is only 10%, the rest EMI, and land keeps rising, so
waiting is the expensive choice. UGC advisor delivers it as friendly, insider advice.

## CONCEPT
**"Bhai, flat mat le — abhi"** (UGC, Omni Flash, 30s). A young property advisor,
mid-action on the actual plot, talks you out of a depreciating flat and into a
clear-title plot you can book for 10%. Feels like a friend giving you a real tip.

## SCRIPT (30s UGC, Hinglish, ~90 words)
Register: single creator to camera, phone energy, one light disfluency allowed.
```

         Zameen? Ulta — badhti hai."

         clear title, sirf atharah lakh se."
```
